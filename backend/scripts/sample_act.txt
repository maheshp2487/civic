THE MODEL TENANCY ACT, 2021

CHAPTER I
PRELIMINARY

Section 1: Short title, extent and commencement.
(1) This Act may be called the Model Tenancy Act, 2021.
(2) It extends to the whole State/Union Territory.
(3) It shall come into force on such date as the State Government/Union Territory Administration may, by notification in the Official Gazette, appoint.

Section 2: Definitions.
In this Act, unless the context otherwise requires,—
(a) “agreement” or “tenancy agreement” means the written agreement executed by the landlord and the tenant as required under this Act;
(b) “landlord” whether called landowner or lessor or by any other name, means a person who receives or is entitled to receive, the rent of any premises.

CHAPTER II
TENANCY

Section 3: Tenancy Agreement.
Notwithstanding anything contained in this Act or any other law for the time being in force, no person shall, after the commencement of this Act, let or take on rent any premises except by an agreement in writing.

Section 4: Period of Tenancy.
(1) All tenancies entered into after the commencement of this Act shall be for a period as agreed between the landlord and the tenant.
(2) The tenant may request the landlord for renewal or extension of the tenancy.
